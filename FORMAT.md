# File format

What ScatterSteg writes into an image, exactly enough to write a compatible decoder. Until the first release it may still change: an image made by one commit may not open with a later one. The implementations are `codec.c` (C) and `rust/src/codec.rs` (Rust), and `tests/test_cli.py` checks both against an independent Python decoder.

## Layout

The image contains no header or version marker, as either would reveal the presence of a message. Every pixel holds one bit per sample, in the LSBs of R, G and B (or of the gray value), set by exchanging values between pairs of samples where possible and LSB matching otherwise. Below, "sample k" means bit position k counted along the payload order: pixel `order[k / channels]`, channel `k % channels`.

1. **Salt** — 16 random bytes, carried as the STC syndrome (as in step 5, with the same costs) of the samples of a public *salt region*: the first k pixels of a public pixel order, `prng_permutation` keyed with `BLAKE2b-256("ScatterSteg v2 salt positions")`, where k = max(⌈128 / channels⌉, min(⌈8192 / channels⌉, ⌊pixels / 16⌋)) and the first min(k · channels, 64 · 128) of its samples are used. Public, so the decoder can find it before it has a key; syndrome-coded, so the roughly 18 changes it needs are as sparse as the payload's.
2. **Keys** — `master = Argon2id(passphrase, salt, opslimit 3, memlimit 256 MiB)`; the scatter key and encryption key are `crypto_kdf` subkeys 1 and 2 of `master`, context `ScatStg2`.
3. **Order** — `prng_permutation(scatter key)` over the pixels, with the salt pixels removed.
4. **Length** — the box length L (32 bits, little-endian), in the LSBs of samples 0–31.
5. **Box** — `nonce (24) || crypto_secretbox(encoding (1) || body)`, as bits (LSB first), is the STC syndrome of the LSBs of the samples from 32 on. With N samples left and m = 8L message bits, n = min(N, 64·m) of them are used; message bit i is row i of H·y, where block i covers samples ⌊i·n/m⌋ to ⌊(i+1)·n/m⌋ − 1 and its j-th sample contributes column j of the 7-row submatrix to rows i…i+6. Columns: splitmix64 seeded with `0x5354432d48415431 ^ (⌊n/m⌋ + 1)`, low 7 bits, with bits 0 and 6 set. The rows run in independent chunks of ⌊2²⁰ / (⌊n/m⌋ + 1)⌋ message bits.

**The message** is carried in a *record*: `name length (1) || file name || message`, with a name length of 0 for no name. The name, when there is one, is a bare file name of 1–255 bytes: no `/`, no control characters (below 0x20, and 0x7F), not `.` or `..`. The secretbox's plaintext is `encoding (1) || body`: with encoding 1, the body is the record as raw DEFLATE (RFC 1951, no zlib or gzip wrapper), used when that is shorter than the record; with encoding 0, the body is the record as is. Any other encoding, a body that isn't exactly one complete DEFLATE stream or that inflates past 64 MiB + 256 bytes, a name length past the record's end, or a name that breaks the rules above is malformed, and decoders refuse it. An encoder may compress however it likes; ScatterSteg uses zlib, at level 9 unless `-z` says otherwise.

**JPEG** uses the same layout over a different kind of position: each *carrier* is one AC coefficient with |value| ≥ 2, taken in component, block-row, block and coefficient order, and carries one bit. Its salt region is counted in carriers (one sample each). DC coefficients, zeros and ±1 are never used, and embedding keeps every carrier at |value| ≥ 2 and within ±1023, so the decoder finds the same carriers and the encoder can write them.

**Costs** determine which samples change and are chosen by the encoder alone (the decoder does not use them). PNG uses HILL: per channel, the reciprocal of the 3×3 mean of the absolute KB high-pass residual, smoothed by a 15×15 mean, so a change is cheap where the image is busy. JPEG uses UERD: the coefficient's quantization step over the energy of its block (the sum of its dequantized AC magnitudes) plus a quarter of each of its eight neighbours', ×1.75 when |value| = 2. Builds made for benchmarks can use other models instead (WOW or S-UNIWARD for PNG, J-UNIWARD for JPEG; see `distortion.h`); as the decoder never sees the costs, what they write reads back with any build.

`prng_permutation(key, n)` is a forward Fisher–Yates shuffle (for `i` from 0 to n−2, swap slot `i` with `i + uniform(n − i)`), with `uniform` drawing little-endian 32-bit words from the ChaCha20-IETF keystream of `key` (zero nonce, counter from 0) and rejecting values below `2³² mod bound`.
